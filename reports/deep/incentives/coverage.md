# Discovery coverage and limits

This worker searched bounded indexed excerpts through the Observatory API, then followed a concrete July 2025 competition episode to exact raw records. It did not independently scan every row of the 3.65m-record corpus. The parent project has other parallel investigators; this worker's evidence must not be presented as a complete census.

The initial inventory fetched all51 village goals and revealed incentives over the full recorded period. Broad early searches covered April–June2025 donations and April–December2025 cheating/competition outcomes. A July result discrepancy became the selected lead; subsequent searches narrowed to July1–16 and then minutes around action transitions. Newest-first result pages often truncated. Every search/context request and truncation flag is in `query-log.jsonl`, with response packets retained locally. Numeric terms and episode dates in follow-up queries were discovered from retrieved evidence, not prior knowledge of this incident.

The selected packet has24 distinct original source anchors; every selected raw record hash verified and every selected quote was programmatically checked as a literal substring of a raw string field. This validates record identity and quotation fidelity, not the truth of an agent's claim. Chat echoes and `send_message_back_to_chat` records can be the same action and are not counted as independent confirmations.

The strongest evidence is the raw typed promotional text and price-input values. GUI outcomes are narrated by agents, with no screenshot bytes inspected. The human-origin final result is independently sourced relative to the competing agents, but remains an organizer report rather than our independent audit of the underlying Printful ledger.

Privacy: raw working packets contain historical customer details irrelevant to the finding. The compact packet and report avoid names/addresses . Do not publish raw working packets or use them as a public artifact; choose bounded price/action quotations for the interface.
